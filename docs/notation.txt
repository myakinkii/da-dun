NOTATION GUIDE

FILE LAYOUT
  title Song name         optional
  tempo 120               quarter notes per minute (default 120)
  time 4/4                time signature (default 4/4)
  # comment               a # at the start of a line or after a space
  instrument: notes ...   one part per line; repeat the name to continue it
  piano (left): ...       same instrument, separate part

NOTES
  c d e f g a b           note names
  c#  eb  bb              # is sharp, b after a letter is flat
  r                       rest
  R                       rest until the end of the bar
  g'  g,                  one octave up / down from where it would land
  g''                     two octaves up (same for ,,)

  Each note lands in the octave closest to the previous note
  (at most 3 letter steps away). Parts start near middle C;
  bass instruments start two octaves lower.
  o3                      reset the reference octave (middle C is o4)

DURATIONS
  1 2 4 8 16 32           whole, half, quarter, eighth, 16th, 32nd
  4.  8..                 dotted, double dotted
  8t  4t  16t             triplets: three notes in the time of two
                          (8t 8t 8t fills one beat)

  Durations stick:  c8 d e f  is four eighth notes.
  A part starts with quarter notes. R doesn't change the duration.

EXPRESSION
  >c                      accent (louder)
  c2~ c8                  tie: one note held for both lengths
  [c e g]2                chord; the next note follows its first note

BARS
  |                       bar line. Bars are checked against the time
                          signature; a short first bar is fine (pickup).
  a'4 R | e R |           one note per bar, then silence

DRUMS
  kick:  |x...x...x...x...|   one character per 16th note
  x  hit    X  accented hit    .  or  -  silence

  Names: kick, snare, clap, hat, open hat, pedal hat, crash, ride,
  ride bell, rim, tom, high tom, mid tom, low tom, floor tom,
  cowbell, tambourine, shaker, claves, or any General MIDI drum name.

INSTRUMENTS
  piano, electric piano, harpsichord, bells, organ, guitar,
  electric guitar, muted guitar, distortion, bass, synth bass,
  strings, pizzicato, harp, choir, trumpet, trombone, horn, brass,
  sax, flute, lead, saw lead, pad, or any General MIDI instrument
  name (a unique part of one works too, like "cello").

EXAMPLE
  title Beethoven 5, opening
  tempo 180
  time 2/4

  strings: r8 g' g g | >eb2 | r8 f f f | >d2~ | d2
  cello:   r8 g g g | >eb2 | r8 f f f | >d2~ | d2

  drums, one bar of a basic rock groove:
  kick:  |x.......x.x.....|
  snare: |....x.......x...|
  hat:   |x.x.x.x.x.x.x.x.|
