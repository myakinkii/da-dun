NOTATION GUIDE

FILE LAYOUT
  title Song name         optional
  tempo 120               quarter notes per minute (default 120)
  time 4/4                time signature (default 4/4)
  key A                   optional key: key A, key F# minor, key Bb, key Am.
                          The editor names the chords and numbers them (I, vi,
                          IV, V) when there is one. It doesn't change any note.
  # comment               a # at the start of a line or after a space
  instrument: notes ...   one part per line; repeat the name to continue it
  piano (left): ...       same instrument, separate part
  piano (left) o3: ...    this line is in octave 3 (see OCTAVES)

NOTES
  c d e f g a b           note names
  c#  eb  bb              # is sharp, b after a letter is flat
  r                       rest
  R                       rest until the end of the bar

OCTAVES
  Every note is in its line's octave: octave 4 (middle C up to B)
  unless the part line names another, like "piano (left) o3:".
  Bass instruments default to octave 2.
  g'  g,                  this note one octave up / down
  g''  g,,                two octaves up / down
  o5                      the rest of this line is in octave 5

  Octaves count from C, as in pitch names: in octave 4, b is B4 but
  c is C4, below it. Pick each line's octave where most of its notes
  are, so few notes need marks:
    piano (right) o5: c8t d e~ e2 f#,8t g#, b, |

DURATIONS
  1 2 4 8 16 32           whole, half, quarter, eighth, 16th, 32nd
  4.  8..                 dotted, double dotted
  8t  4t  16t             triplets: three notes in the time of two
                          (8t 8t 8t fills one beat)

  Durations stick:  c8 d e f  is four eighth notes.
  A part starts with quarter notes. R doesn't change the duration.

EXPRESSION
  >c                      accent (louder)
  c2~ c8                  tie: one note held for both lengths
  [c e g]2                chord; the next note follows its first note
  >[c e g]2               accented chord

BARS
  |                       bar line. Bars are checked against the time
                          signature; a short first bar is fine (pickup).
  a'4 R | e R |           one note per bar, then silence
  @15                     this part continues from bar 15 (rests until then),
                          handy for parts that come in late:
                            bass: @15 a,1 | a1 |
                          also works in drum lines:  kick: @9 |x...x...x...x...|

DRUMS
  One line per drum sound, in either of two ways. The editor reads each
  line as a step grid first, and as notes if the grid doesn't fit.

  Step grid, for straight grooves:
    kick:  |x...x...x...x...|   one character per 16th note
    x  hit    X  accented hit    .  or  -  silence

  Notes, for anything else (triplets, swing, odd rhythms):
    kick:  x r x r |            hits on beats 1 and 3
    ride:  x4 x4t x8t x4 x4t x8t |   swung: each 4t 8t is long-short
    x  hit    X  or  >x  accented hit    r  R  rests
    Durations work as in melodies and carry over from line to line.

  Both kinds of line take | bar lines and @N, and a part can mix them,
  one kind per line.

  Names: kick, snare, clap, hat, open hat, pedal hat, crash, ride,
  ride bell, rim, tom, high tom, mid tom, low tom, floor tom,
  cowbell, tambourine, shaker, claves, or any General MIDI drum name.

INSTRUMENTS
  piano, electric piano, harpsichord, bells, organ, guitar,
  electric guitar, muted guitar, distortion, bass, synth bass,
  strings, pizzicato, harp, choir, trumpet, trombone, horn, brass,
  sax, flute, lead, saw lead, pad, or any General MIDI instrument
  name (a unique part of one works too, like "cello").

EXAMPLE
  title Beethoven 5, opening
  tempo 180
  time 2/4

  strings:  r8 g g g | >eb2 | r8 f f f | >d2~ | d2
  cello o3: r8 g g g | >eb2 | r8 f f f | >d2~ | d2

  drums, one bar of a basic rock groove:
  kick:  |x.......x.x.....|
  snare: |....x.......x...|
  hat:   |x.x.x.x.x.x.x.x.|
